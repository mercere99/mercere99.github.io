Games and Puzzles in Development

These are some of the games and puzzles I'm working on. Each is playable in your browser. They're works in progress, so expect changes as development continues.

Ring Charge

A ring-merging puzzle on a hexagonal board. Place tiles, match the colors of outer rings, and pull their dots together to charge and clear rings. Build cascades, unlock spaces, and reach each level's score goal.

Play Ring Charge

Wizard Crisis

A dungeon crawler where you explore, battle monsters, collect equipment, and learn spells. Develop your wizard's abilities as you descend, with sliding walls and pits adding puzzles along the way.

Play Wizard Crisis

Knock It Off

A cat physics puzzle starring a small pink agent of chaos. Shove objects onto paw prints, push things off edges, and work your way through the levels.

Play Knock It Off

Logic Puzzles

Explore four logic puzzles with pencil marks, hints, and difficulty profiles.

Sudoku

Fill the grid so each row, column, and box contains the digits 1–9 without repeats.

Play Sudoku

Kakuro

A number crossword: fill each run with digits 1–9 that add up to its clue, without repeating a digit within the run.

Play Kakuro

Slitherlink

Draw a single closed loop along the grid's edges. Each number tells you how many of the edges around its cell belong to the loop.

Play Slitherlink

ShepherdLink

Fence the flock with a single closed loop that satisfies the number clues, keeping every sheep inside and every wolf outside.

Play ShepherdLink
